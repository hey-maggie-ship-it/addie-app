Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Ankora ("we," "us," or "our") is a productivity assistant built for people who want clarity on what to do next. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, we collect your email address via a magic link. We don't store passwords.
  • App data. Tasks, grocery lists, and your onboarding preferences (communication style, patterns, and context) are stored so your data syncs across devices.
  • Conversation content. Messages you send to Ankora are processed by the Anthropic API to generate responses. We do not store individual conversation messages server-side beyond what is needed to produce a reply.
  • Payment information. If you subscribe, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of your subscription status but never see or store your card details.
  • Usage data. We track a daily message count per account to enforce the free-tier limit. We do not sell this data.

2. How we use it

  • To provide and sync your tasks, lists, and preferences across devices.
  • To personalize Ankora's tone based on your profile settings.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription.
  • To enforce fair usage limits on the free tier.
  • To send transactional emails (sign-in links, receipts). We don't send marketing email without your consent.

3. Third-party services

Ankora is built on services that have their own privacy practices:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Anthropic — AI processing of your messages.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Vercel — hosting and serverless functions.

We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. You can delete all your data at any time from the app's Settings → "Clear all data." To permanently delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

Ankora is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact
